CRS Entity Tax Residency Self-Certification — Instructions

Entities Only ▼
Instructions

Regulations based on the OECD Common Reporting Standard ("CRS") require Maktubs to collect and report certain information about an account holder's tax residency. If the account holder's tax residence is located outside El Salvador, we may be legally obliged to pass on the information in this form and other financial information to the tax authorities of El Salvador, and they may exchange this information with tax authorities of another jurisdiction pursuant to intergovernmental agreements to exchange financial account information.

This form will remain valid unless there is a change in circumstances that makes this form incorrect or incomplete. In that case you must notify us and provide an updated self-certification.

Please complete this form where you need to self-certify on behalf of an entity account holder. If you are an individual account holder or sole trader, please complete an Individual tax residency self-certification form instead.

For joint or multiple account holders, please complete a separate form for each account holder.

If the Account Holder is a U.S. tax resident, you should indicate that on this form and you may also need to fill in an IRS W-9 form. For more information on tax residence, please consult your tax adviser or the information at the OECD automatic exchange of information portal.

Where the Account Holder is a Passive NFE, or an Investment Entity located in a Non-Participating Jurisdiction managed by another Financial Institution, please provide information on the natural person(s) who exercise control over the Account Holder by completing a Controlling Person tax residency self-certification form for each Controlling Person.

Entity Types

Financial Institution – Investment Entity: An entity that primarily conducts as a business trading in money market instruments, individual and collective portfolio management, or otherwise investing, administering, or managing Financial Assets on behalf of other persons.

Active NFE: A Non-Financial Entity meeting any of the following criteria: less than 50% passive income and less than 50% passive assets; publicly traded; government entity; start-up; in liquidation; non-profit organisation.

Passive NFE: Any NFE that is not an Active NFE. An Investment Entity located in a Non-Participating Jurisdiction managed by another Financial Institution is also treated as a Passive NFE for purposes of the CRS.

Controlling Persons: The natural person(s) who exercise control over an entity, typically those holding 25% or more ownership interest. In the case of a trust, Controlling Persons include settlors, trustees, protectors, and beneficiaries.

TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number): A unique combination of letters or numbers assigned by a jurisdiction to identify an individual or entity for tax purposes. If a TIN is unavailable, provide Reason A (jurisdiction does not issue TINs), Reason B (unable to obtain TIN), or Reason C (no TIN required by domestic law).

Declaration

By signing the CRS Entity Self-Certification form you declare that all statements made are, to the best of your knowledge and belief, correct and complete. You undertake to advise Maktubs within 30 days of any change in circumstances which affects the tax residency status of the Account Holder identified in the form, and to provide a suitably updated self-certification within 30 days of such change in circumstances.
