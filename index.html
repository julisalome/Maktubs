Customer Identification Program (CIP)

To support Government initiatives in combating terrorist financing and money laundering activities, AML regulations mandate that all financial institutions must acquire, authenticate and maintain customer-related information. At the time of opening an account, we shall ask your name, physical address, date of birth and other information which shall allow us to verify your identity. We shall also request your passport and other identity documents. You agree to use the Account and/or the services described in this Agreement only for legitimate purposes and according to the applicable laws and regulations, including those related to preventing money laundering and terrorist financing. The account holder acknowledges that, in order to comply with the internal policies of the Company and/or the applicable provisions of the law or administrative regulations of El Salvador, the Company could be bound to withhold or freeze any funds and/or transfer to the relevant authorities.

Definitions

Account: Means the deposit account opened by a Deposit Account Application.

Accountholder / Client / You / Your: The corporation, partnership, sole proprietorship, unincorporated association, trust, estate, or other business entity which has a deposit account at the Company.

Available Balance: Funds in your account available for withdrawal in accordance with the Company's Funds Availability Disclosure.

Business Day: Any day except Saturdays, Sundays, Holidays, and Public Holidays where the Company is required by Salvadoran law to close.

Cash: Means the currency of legal tender in the United States of America or any other currency in which any account is denominated.

Chequing Account: An account which transactions are subject to payment orders and wire transfer instructions.

Fixed Time Deposit: A Fixed Time Account shall have the characteristics defined in this Agreement.

Funds Transfer: A wire or other electronic transfer of funds.

Saving Account: An account set up for the accumulation of funds and which the Company reserves the right of prior notice before any withdrawal of funds.

Account Opening

The accountholder shall be obligated to the applicable disclosure provisions of the Deposit Account Application of the type of account requested. The account can be opened individually, jointly, or separately. The accountholder authorizes the Company to conduct any credit, bank, personal or business verification deemed appropriate for opening an account. The Company reserves the right, at its own discretion, to close any account without prior notice when the accountholder does not provide the documentation requested.

Deposits

Deposits can be made via funds transfer. The Company shall not accept any cash deposit. Upon receipt of a deposit, the depositor shall check to ensure that all information presented is correct. The account shall be adjusted in case of errors and the depositor must inform the Company of any errors within 60 days of making the deposit.

Withdrawals

The Company reserves the right to deny any outgoing transfers from the Client's account if the Company determines that the Client has outstanding debts on any product offered. This restriction applies when the amount of the Client's debts exceeds the funds that will be available after the proposed transfer is processed. The Company will notify the Client of any denied transfers, providing details of the outstanding debts and the resulting balance.

Interests on Account

The Company shall pay interest on balances in accounts that earn them, at the rates and under the terms and conditions periodically to be disclosed. Interest is compounded monthly. The accountholder must maintain a daily balance equal to or greater than $0.01 to earn the interest set by the Company. If the accountholder closes the account before interest is credited, the accountholder shall not receive the accrued interest.

Statements

The Company shall have statements available to the accountholder at any time through the Company's website. The accountholder shall promptly examine the statement and notify the Company of any errors or unauthorized charges within a period of sixty (60) calendar days from the date of the statement. Failure to notify within this timeframe will result in such claims being deemed waived and time-barred.

Inactive Accounts and Unclaimed Funds

Any deposit account which for a continuous period of six months has no activity shall be automatically inactivated and no transaction shall be accepted unless previously authorized by the Company. The Company may charge inactivity fees on the account until the account is active or until the account's funds are sent to the entity entitled to receive such unclaimed funds.

Closing the Account

The Company reserves the right to close the account at any time by sending a notice to the accountholder by email within a period of five (5) calendar days. The Company reserves the right to close the account immediately without notice in the case of suspected fraud or mismanagement.

Limitation of Liability

You agree that in any litigation, your sole right to any relief shall be limited to breach of contract. You specifically waive any and all claims based on negligence, gross negligence, wilful misconduct, breach of fiduciary duty, or commercial unreasonableness. In no event shall you claim or shall the Company be liable for special, punitive, indirect or consequential damages.

Applicable Laws

You agree that the Account and this Agreement will be governed by the laws and regulations of El Salvador. You further acknowledge and agree that funds deposited in the account are not insured by the Government of El Salvador or other insurance companies.

Section II: Joint and Indistinct Accounts

The Company may open joint accounts (AND) in certain situations. If it is a joint account, all the accountholders authorize two or more of them to sign on the account and shall require these people signing together for a transaction. In the case of indistinct accounts (AND/OR), the Company shall pay or deliver the total or part of the funds in the account to any of the accountholders. Every accountholder has the right to deposit and withdraw funds, to agree special arrangements related to the account, and close the account.

Section III: Checking Accounts

A checking account can be opened individually, jointly, or indistinct. Deposits can be made via funds transfer. The Company shall not accept deposits in cash. The accountholder cannot turn payment orders against uncollected funds and the Company reserves the right to refuse payment orders drawn against uncollected funds.

Section IV: Savings Accounts

Savings accounts are interest-bearing deposits for personal use. The accountholder is not allowed more than five (5) transfers or withdrawals in each month. No checks shall be issued for this account. The Company reserves the right to request at least seven (7) days advance notice before any withdrawal of the whole or partial balance.

Section V: Fixed Time Deposit

A fixed time deposit account can be opened individually, jointly, or separately. The fixed time deposits at Maktubs are not negotiable or transferable instruments. Any partial or total prepayment of a fixed time deposit before maturity carries a penalty of 90 days of interest for a fixed term of 364 days or less, or 180 days of interest for fixed periods of 365 days or more. The fixed time deposit shall automatically renew at maturity with a grace period of 10 calendar days to withdraw funds without penalty.

Section VI: Online Banking

Online banking services available include: online access to accounts, access to statements, and wire transfers. The accountholder has the option of consulting monthly statements through online banking.

Force Majeure

The Company shall not be liable for any failure to perform any obligation under this Agreement if such failure is due to legal or governmental constraint, interruption of transmission or communication facilities, equipment failure, act of God, war, civil disorder, terrorist acts, strikes, or other events beyond the Company's reasonable control.

CRS Entity Tax Residency Self-Certification / Autocertificación de Residencia Fiscal

Entities Only ▾
Instructions / Instrucciones

Regulations based on the OECD Common Reporting Standard ("CRS") require Maktubs to collect and report certain information about an account holder's tax residency. If the account holder's tax residence is located outside El Salvador, we may be legally obliged to pass on the information in this form and other financial information to the tax authorities of El Salvador, and they may exchange this information with tax authorities of another jurisdiction pursuant to intergovernmental agreements to exchange financial account information.

This form will remain valid unless there is a change in circumstances relating to information, such as the account holder's tax status, that makes this form incorrect or incomplete. In that case you must notify us and provide an updated self-certification.

Please complete this form where you need to self-certify on behalf of an entity account holder. If you are an individual account holder or sole trader, please complete an "Individual tax residency self-certification form" instead.

For joint or multiple account holders, please complete a separate form for each account holder.

If the Account Holder is a U.S. tax resident, you should indicate that on this form and you may also need to fill in an IRS W-9 form.

Entity Types / Tipos de Entidad

Financial Institution – Investment Entity: An entity that primarily conducts trading in money market instruments, individual and collective portfolio management, or otherwise investing, administering, or managing Financial Assets on behalf of other persons.

Active NFE: A Non-Financial Entity that meets certain criteria — e.g., less than 50% passive income, publicly traded, government entity, start-up, or non-profit.

Passive NFE: Any NFE that is not an Active NFE.

Controlling Person: The natural person(s) who exercise control over an entity, typically those holding 25% or more ownership. In the case of a trust, includes settlors, trustees, protectors, and beneficiaries.
